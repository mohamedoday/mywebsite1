THE BIG PICTURE

First, a little context.

00 — 15

Every page you visit is built on a foundation called HTML. It tells a browser what things are: a headline, a photograph, a link, a form. Think of it as the structure of a house—before the paint, the furniture, or the mood lighting.

In this course, you won't just memorize tags. You'll learn to make thoughtful, usable pages—and understand how your work fits into the wonderfully weird, ever-growing web.
